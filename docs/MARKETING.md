# Launch marketing

Plan and ready-to-use text for launching TailTick on X and YouTube Shorts, and for asking
pet accounts on X to share it. Replace `[link]` with the App Store link once the app is live
(see "Links that show what works" for the per-channel version).

## The message

**One line:** TailTick is a shared checklist for your pet's day: everyone sees what's done,
who did it and what was missed.

**The problem it solves**, in the words people use:
- "Did anyone feed the dog?" / "Did you give him his medicine?" "I thought you did."
- Feeding twice, or not at all, when two people share the care.
- Not knowing what the sitter or walker actually did while you're away.

**Who it's for:** couples and families sharing a pet, people who use a sitter or walker,
pets on medicine, owners who travel.

**Lead with the problem, then show the app.** People scroll past feature lists but stop for
"who fed the dog?".

## Before launch (while the app is in review)

- [ ] **Profiles**: an X account and a YouTube channel named TailTick (handle e.g. `@TailTickApp`
      if `@TailTick` is taken). Use `design/TailTick-AppIcon-1024.png` as the picture, the
      one-line message as the bio, and https://abhay244.github.io/PawDone/ as the website.
- [ ] **Record the demo footage** (see "Demo video").
- [ ] **Build a list of 30–50 accounts** to contact (see "Asking pet accounts to share").
- [ ] **Post once about being in review** (below). It costs nothing and warms people up.
- [ ] **Download the official "Download on the App Store" badge** from Apple's marketing
      guidelines page (developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines). Use it unchanged.

Don't post the App Store link or say "available now" until you've released the app and
downloaded it yourself.

**Habits that help on X:**
- Reply to every comment in the first hour; replies are what spread a post.
- Spend 15 minutes a day replying usefully to pet posts (not with the link). People check your
  profile and find the pinned post.

- Post when both India and the US are awake: around 8–10 PM IST is morning in the US.

## Demo video

One 30–40 second vertical video works for the X launch post, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

**Record it on your iPhone with the ready-made demo account**, so the screens look full and
the camera works for photo proof:

1. In Xcode: **Product → Scheme → Edit Scheme → Run → Arguments**, add `-PawDoneScreenshots`
   under "Arguments Passed On Launch".
2. Run the app on your iPhone. It opens signed in as Maya with Bruno the dog, two helpers,
   Premium and a month of history. Nothing reaches the server and it resets on every launch.
   To use your own pet's name, edit the pet in Account first.
3. Turn on **Do Not Disturb**, then record with **Control Center → Screen Recording**.
4. Afterwards, untick the argument again so normal runs use your real account.

**Shot list:**

| Time | Show | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Your pet by its bowl, or the Today list | "Did anyone feed the dog?" |
| 3–12 s | Today: tap a task, take a photo of your pet, Done | "Tick it off, with photo proof" |
| 12–18 s | Today with names next to done tasks | "See who did what" |
| 18–25 s | Reports: tap a day with a miss | "See what was missed" |
| 25–32 s | Account → Invite someone, the code appears | "Share with family and sitters" |
| 32–38 s | The icon, "TailTick" and the App Store badge | "Free on iPhone" |

**Editing** (CapCut or iMovie are enough): export 1080×1920; add captions, since most people
watch muted; add light, royalty-free music; keep cuts fast, 2–5 seconds each; show the
problem in the first 2 seconds.

## YouTube Shorts

Same vertical format, 20–40 seconds, one idea each, hook in the first 2 seconds. End with
"Search TailTick on the App Store" on screen. Links in Shorts descriptions and comments
aren't clickable, so put the App Store link on the channel profile, where it is.

1. **"Who fed the dog?"** Two people both reach for the food bowl, or both say "I fed him".
   Cut to the app: one taps Breakfast done, the other sees "Sam at 8:08 AM".
   *Title:* Who fed the dog? 🐶 This app ends the argument
2. **"Pet sitter with photo proof."** Leaving for a trip, the sitter ticks off tasks with
   photos, you check from the airport. *Title:* How I know my pet sitter actually walked my dog
3. **"Never miss the meds."** A reminder notification arrives, tap, tick off "Medicine".
   *Title:* The easiest way to remember your pet's medicine
4. **"I built an app for my pet."** You, talking to camera for 20 seconds: why you built it,
   then a quick tour. Build-in-public stories do well. *Title:* I built an iPhone app for my pet
5. **"A month of pet care in one screen."** Scroll the Reports calendar, tap a missed day.
   *Title:* Tracking every walk and meal for a month

Post one every 2–3 days; small, steady channels grow faster than one big upload.

## Asking pet accounts to share

**Who:** pet owners, pet sitters, dog walkers and vet techs on X with roughly
2,000–50,000 followers. Smaller accounts reply more often, cost less (often nothing) and
their followers trust them. Look for recent posts with real replies, not just likes. Aim for
India and the US.

**Finding them:** X search for "dog mom", "cat dad", "pet sitter", "dog walker", `#dogsofX`,
`#catsofX`; check who your first followers follow.

**What to offer:** free Premium so they can add their partner or sitter. Once the
subscriptions are approved, create **offer codes** in App Store Connect (Subscriptions →
a plan → Offer Codes), e.g. 3 months free. They redeem in the App Store app (profile →
Redeem Gift Card or Code); if Premium doesn't show in TailTick straight away, they tap
**Restore purchases** on the Premium tab.

**First message** (personalise the first line every time; identical messages get flagged as
spam, so send 10–15 a day at most):

```
Hi {name}! Loved your post about {specific post}. 🐾

I'm a solo developer and just launched TailTick, a free iPhone app where a household shares their pet's daily care: tick off walks, meals and meds with photo proof, and see who did what.

Would you like to try it? I can give you 3 months of Premium so you can add your family or sitter. No obligation to post, but if you like it, a mention would mean a lot.
```

**If they ask about payment**, or you want to offer it:

```
Do you do paid shoutouts? I'd love one post with a short screen recording. What would it cost?
```

**Follow-up**, once, after 4–5 days:

```
Hi {name}, just bumping this in case it got buried. No worries at all if it's not for you!
```

**Disclosure:** if they get anything for the post (money or free Premium), they should label
it, e.g. "#ad" or "gifted". Advertising rules in India (ASCI) and the US (FTC) require it.

**Keep a simple sheet:** handle, followers, niche, date contacted, reply, posted (link),
installs from their link.

## Links that show what works

App Store Connect can make a link per channel and count the installs from each: in the app's
**Analytics** tab, look for **Campaigns** and **Generate campaign link** (available once the
app is live; Apple moves this menu now and then). Use one per channel, e.g. `x-launch`, `yt-shorts`, and one per influencer. After a
week you'll know where to spend your time.

## Launch day checklist

1. Release the app, download it from the App Store and check sign-in and the paywall prices.
2. Make the campaign links.
3. Post the launch video and thread on X; pin it.
4. Upload Short #1.
5. Send the first 10–15 messages.
6. Reply to everything for the rest of the day.

Then for two weeks: a Short every 2–3 days, a post every couple of days, 10–15 messages a
day, and fix anything users report quickly. Early users who feel heard become the ones who
recommend the app.
