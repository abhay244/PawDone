Getting started

How do I set up my pet's schedule?

After signing in, add your pet and choose from suggested daily tasks. Add or change tasks anytime in the Schedule tab. Changes apply from today; past days keep their history.

Why isn't a task I just added showing today?

If you add a task after its time has already passed today, it starts tomorrow, so it doesn't appear as missed straight away.

How do I mark a task done?

On the Today tab, tap the task, optionally take a photo and add a note, then tap Done. You can undo your own check-off until the end of the day.

When is a task counted as missed?

If nobody marks it done by midnight in your pet's time zone. You can see each day's results in the Reports tab.

Family and helpers

How do I invite someone?

Go to Account → Family & helpers → Invite someone and share the 8-character code. They install TailTick, choose Join a household and enter it. Codes work once, for 7 days. Inviting needs Premium, and you can invite up to two people.

What can helpers do?

They see your pet's day and reports, mark tasks done with photos and notes, and get reminders. Only you can change the pet's details and schedule.

How do I remove someone or leave a household?

Owners: in Account → Family & helpers, swipe left on the person and tap Remove. Helpers: Account → Leave household.

Notifications

I'm not getting reminders
  1. Check notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings → Notifications → TailTick.
  2. In TailTick, check Account → Reminders is on, and the task's own Reminder switch is on in Schedule.
  3. Reminders aren't sent for tasks that are already done.
  4. Open the app once after reinstalling or signing in, so it can register for notifications.

Account

Why was I signed out on my other phone?

Free accounts can be used on one phone at a time. Signing in on a new phone signs out the old one. Premium accounts can be used on all your devices.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your pet and its schedule, history and photos. If you have Premium, cancel the subscription first (see below); deleting the account doesn't cancel it. If you can't use the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Premium and billing

What does Premium include?

Inviting up to two family members or helpers, shared photo proof that everyone can see, "task done" notifications for the household, and using TailTick on all your devices.

How do I cancel?

In TailTick, go to Account → Manage subscription, or on iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → TailTick. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Premium but it isn't active

Open the paywall (Account → Get TailTick Premium) and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If it still doesn't work, email us.

What happens to helpers if Premium ends?

Their access is paused, and your data is kept. When you renew, they get access back automatically.
