# YouTube Shorts

Three 20–27 second vertical videos (1080×1920) with captions, a voiceover and background
music, made from recordings of the app. The built videos are in `output/` (not in git).

| Video | Hook |
|---|---|
| `1-who-fed-the-dog.mp4` | Who fed the dog? Shared checklist, done notifications, family |
| `2-never-miss-the-meds.mp4` | Medicine: schedule once, reminder, tick it off |
| `3-did-the-sitter-do-it.mp4` | Pet sitter: who did what, missed tasks, helpers can't change the schedule |

Upload them only after the app is live: they end with "Free on the App Store".

## Uploading

In the YouTube app, tap **+ → Upload a video** (videos under 3 minutes in portrait become
Shorts). Links in Shorts descriptions aren't clickable, so put the App Store link on your
channel profile and say "Search TailTick on the App Store".

## Changing and rebuilding

Scripts, captions and clip timings are at the top of `make-shorts.swift`. From this folder:

```bash
python3 make-music.py music.wav 60
swift make-shorts.swift clips music.wav
```

The voice is macOS's built-in **Samantha**. For a more natural voice, download a Premium one
(for example "Ava (Premium)") in System Settings → Accessibility → the spoken content / Read &
Speak settings → System voice → Manage Voices, set `voiceName` in `make-shorts.swift`, and
rebuild. The music is generated by `make-music.py`, so there are no music rights to clear; you
can also swap it for a track from YouTube's free Audio Library when uploading.

## Re-recording the clips

The clips in `clips/` are recordings of the screenshot demo on the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator.
Launch the Debug app with `-PawDoneScreenshots -PawDoneDemoTime 17:50` (status bar `5:50`) for
`today`, `notify-done`, `tick-walk`, `reports` and `family`, and with
`-PawDoneDemoTime 20:55` (status bar `8:55`) for `notify-due`, `tick-supp` and `schedule`:

```bash
xcrun simctl status_bar booted override --time 5:50 --batteryState discharging --batteryLevel 100 --wifiBars 3 --cellularBars 4 --operatorName ""
xcrun simctl launch booted com.abhaysingh.pawdone -PawDoneScreenshots -PawDoneDemoTime 17:50
xcrun simctl io booted recordVideo --codec=h264 clips/today.mp4   # Ctrl-C to stop
xcrun simctl push booted com.abhaysingh.pawdone clips/push-done.json
```

The notification clips are recorded while sending `push-done.json` or `push-due.json`. After
re-recording, check the cut points (`ranges`) in `make-shorts.swift`.
